The brief

Radiate is about meeting people, but its brand didn’t feel friendly.

Radiate is a real-time social and event map app that connects people going to the same concerts, festivals and nightlife events.

  • Partners120+festival partnerships, including Ultra, Live Nation and Corona
  • Connections30M+real-life connections made through the app
  • Users1M+the largest dedicated festival social app in the US

The old brand felt cold and closed off: dark, technical and hard to approach, for an app whose whole point is helping strangers say hi. The new identity needed to feel warm, open and welcoming, especially to first-timers and people going on their own.

02

Research

A category squeezed between dating apps and event utilities.

  • FeatureTinder Festival ModeA profile badge for an event. No event info, forums or festival partners.
  • Niche appsFestivalMates, FrontstageBetter music-taste matching and European coverage, but much smaller communities.
  • UtilitiesOfficial festival appsSchedules and logistics, with no social layer at all.

The audience is 18 to 30, into EDM, raves, concerts and clubs. They go for the people as much as the music, often solo or with a small crew, and they want to be talked to like a friend hyping up the next show, never like a corporation.

Three values came out of the research:

  • 01BelongingReal, in-the-moment connection with strangers who love the same music. Not long-term algorithm matching.
  • 02The momentWhere you are, where your friends are, what is happening nearby. Right now, not a feed of old posts.
  • 03TrustA PayPal-backed marketplace for tickets and gear with escrow, so nobody gets scammed or stood up.
03

Archetypes & personas

Three ways people arrive, from planner to curious.

  • Belonging and connectionThe Event PlannerPlanning how to go and who to go with. Needs to find others at the same event and coordinate meetups.
  • Excitement and discoveryThe Event ExplorerWants to go out but has not picked an event. Needs to discover events and get a sense of the atmosphere.
  • Comfort and trustThe CuriousInto live music but new to the scene. Needs to know what to expect and to feel welcome.
04

Design tenets

Make Radiate feel like an invitation to find your people through live music.

Belonging · Immediate · Trust · Vibrant · Welcoming · Expressive

  • 01Make belonging visibleShow people connecting through shared music, so first-timers can picture themselves joining.
  • 02Connect the moment to the next stepActive language and clear hierarchy make it obvious what is happening and how to join.
  • 03Let real people shape the brandCandid moments, individual styles and a natural, friendly voice.
  • 04Give the visual energyVibrant colour, dynamic composition and purposeful motion, with enough breathing room to stay readable.
  • 05Create inviting welcomesWarm, conversational invitations that make newcomers and solo attendees comfortable.
  • 06Let individuality showA flexible system for different tastes and styles, around a consistent core.
05

Narrative & mission

Come for the music, and remember the people you shared it with.

At Radiate, connections matter as much as music. We help people discover live events, connect with others going, and turn a conversation into a shared experience. We want a live music community where you feel welcome to show up as yourself, and that belonging grows with every first hello. The music brings us together. Together, we Radiate.

The mission: make live music social for everyone, helping newcomers, solo explorers and organizers find events, connect with others, and turn a conversation into a shared experience.

06

Logo

A mark that bounces like a crowd, in black, white and every accent.

The logo works in black or white on light and blue backgrounds, and in each accent colour. Safe zones are measured with the letter “e” from the wordmark, and there is a minimum size for the mark and for the wordmark.

07

Colour

One deep blue, four loud accents.

Primary blue #0051EE, secondary orange #FF5B23, and three accents: pink #F683FA, lime #D7FC00 and green #21FF7F.

08

Typography

Degular for headings, Tenon for body text.

Headings use Degular Bold, subheadings and smaller headers use Degular Semibold, and body text uses Tenon Light.
