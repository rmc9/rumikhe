Secret Universe.

An interactive digital installation for the Hong Kong McDonald’s 50th Anniversary Exhibition.

This project is an immersive interactive experience featuring three iconic McDonald’s characters, Birdie, Grimace and Hamburglar, blending 3D and 2D generative animation to create a mysterious, explorable digital space.

Visitors encounter the characters within an atmospheric environment. The experience bridges playful nostalgia and motion design, merging the familiar charm of the brand’s mascots with an unexpected sense of wonder.
